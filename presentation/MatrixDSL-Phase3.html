01 · What changed

From working parts to a working compiler

At Review 2 every stage existed and was tested on its own, but nothing joined them up. The Tensor IR was built by hand, and the MDT programs were written by hand. Phase 3 closes every gap between the stages and then measures what the optimisations are worth.

One backend, three windows onto it

    
mtxserve runs the real compiler as a small local web service on 127.0.0.1:8765. The Chrome extension, the full Lab dashboard and this page all send it programs and draw what comes back. Nothing leaves your machine.
